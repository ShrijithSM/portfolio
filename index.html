Feature  ·  No. 01

StockSage

An AI-powered trading terminal

A five-agent CrewAI pipeline powered by LLaMA 4 Scout via the Groq API, scanning the NSE and NYSE autonomously. It delivers real-time BUY / SELL / AVOID calls complete with entry, stop-loss, and ATR targets, streamed over SSE into a React front end with TradingView-style candlestick charts.
